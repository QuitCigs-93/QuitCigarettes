Objective 01 · Reduce smoking

Your health is worth protecting.

Understanding the risks of smoking is an important step towards making healthier choices.

What does smoking do to your health?

Tobacco smoke contains thousands of chemicals. Smoking increases the risk of serious illnesses, including lung cancer, heart disease, strokes and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).

Smoking can also affect circulation, damage the lungs and make existing health conditions worse.

It's never too late to quit.

Stopping smoking can improve your health at any age. The body begins to recover after quitting, and the benefits continue over time.

How can you start quitting?

01. Choose a quit date.
Set a realistic date and prepare for situations that trigger cravings.

02. Get support.
Speak to a pharmacist, GP or local stop-smoking service.

03. Prepare for cravings.
Find activities that distract you and people who can support you.

04. Keep going.
If you experience a setback, don't give up. You can try again.
