Why this campaign matters

Small changes can make a lasting difference.

Clear the Air is a UK awareness campaign aimed at adults aged 40–65. Our mission is to reduce smoking, raise awareness of tobacco pricing and tackle cigarette litter in our communities.

Our campaign objectives

Three goals. One cleaner future.

Reduce smoking
