Objective 02 · Tobacco pricing

Understand the real cost.

Exploring how tobacco taxation and cigarette prices can influence smoking habits.

What is tobacco duty?

Tobacco duty is a tax charged by the UK government on tobacco products. It is one of the measures used in tobacco-control policy.

Why does cigarette pricing matter?

The price of cigarettes can influence whether people start smoking, continue smoking or consider quitting.

Increasing tobacco prices through taxation is one approach governments can use to discourage tobacco consumption, particularly among people who might otherwise begin smoking.

Our campaign objective

Clear the Air aims to raise awareness of tobacco taxation and support discussion about cigarette pricing as part of wider efforts to reduce smoking across the UK.

Why should we consider the wider impact?

Pricing policies should be considered alongside accessible quitting support, particularly for people who already experience nicotine addiction.
