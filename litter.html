Objective 03 · Reduce cigarette litter

Our streets deserve better.

Protecting our environment starts with the small choices we make every day.

Why are cigarette butts a problem?

Cigarette ends are one of the most commonly discarded forms of litter. Many cigarette filters contain cellulose acetate, a type of plastic which does not simply disappear when thrown onto the ground.

Cigarette filters can break down into smaller plastic pieces and release chemicals into the surrounding environment.

Discarded cigarette ends also make streets, parks and public spaces look neglected and create additional work for local street-cleaning services.

Small litter. Bigger consequences.

Even one cigarette butt contributes to unnecessary pollution. Responsible disposal can help keep our communities cleaner.

How can we prevent cigarette litter?

01. Use designated bins.
Use public cigarette bins where they are provided.

02. Carry a portable ashtray.
If there is no suitable bin, use a purpose-made portable ashtray.

03. Dispose of cigarettes safely.
Ensure cigarettes are fully extinguished before disposal.

04. Protect public spaces.
Never throw cigarette ends onto pavements, roads or into drains.

Our environmental mission

Clear the Air wants to encourage individuals, businesses and communities to take responsibility for reducing cigarette litter across the UK.
